Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started
This is my first time using this kiosk. How do I get started?
  1. Click the Teams button above.
  2. Sign in with your Vivie credentials (firstname.lastname@vivie.org).
  3. Enter the initial password provided by your manager or IT during onboarding.
  4. You will be prompted to change your password and set up the Microsoft Authenticator app on your phone.
  5. If you get stuck, submit an IT Support Form or call (320) 762-4300.
For a detailed walkthrough with screenshots, ask your manager for the Vivie Account Setup Guide.
Where is Outlook / email?
As part of a recent update, email is no longer available on kiosk computers. For messaging, use Microsoft Teams by clicking the Teams button above. If you need to reach someone urgently, submit an IT Support Form or call (320) 762-4300.
How do I reset my password?
You can reset your Vivie password at https://aka.ms/sspr from any browser. If you are locked out, submit an IT Support Form or call (320) 762-4300 for assistance.
Can I save files on this kiosk?
No. Kiosk computers do not save files between sessions. Any files downloaded or created will be removed when the session ends. Use OneDrive or SharePoint through Teams to store files securely.
How do I access Eldermark / Service Minder?
Eldermark is available via the Service Minder shortcut on the desktop. Press CTRL + ALT + DELETE on the kiosk and log in as yourself to access the desktop shortcut. If you don't see it, submit an IT Support Form or call (320) 762-4300.
Apps & Tools
What is Point Click Care?
Point Click Care is a cloud-based platform for long-term care management.
What is PCC Point of Care?
PCC Point of Care is a companion application to Point Click Care, designed for bedside documentation and real-time care tracking.
What is UKG?
UKG is Vivie's timekeeping and scheduling platform. Use it to clock in/out, view your schedule, request time off, and check your pay stubs.
What is PalCare?
PalCare (Palatium Care) is a care management platform used for resident documentation and care coordination.
What is EMenu?
EMenu is Vivie's dietary and dining services platform used to manage resident meal planning, menu choices, and food service operations.
What is Worxhub?
Worxhub is a facilities management tool used to submit and track maintenance work orders. Select your community from the Worxhub dropdown above to get started.
How-To
How do I access Microsoft Teams?
Click the Teams button above. Sign in with your Vivie credentials (firstname.lastname@vivie.org). You will be prompted for multi-factor authentication on each kiosk session.
How do I join a Teams meeting?
Click the Teams button above. When the Teams web page opens, find and click your meeting link or open your calendar within Teams to join the meeting.
How do I access The Vine?
Click the Vivie Vine button above. The Vine is Vivie's employee intranet on SharePoint, where you can find company news, tools, and resources.

New to The Vine? Visit the Vine Training Hub for guided video walkthroughs on navigating hubs, communication standards, and using Teams channels.
How do I access Relias Training?
Click the Relias Training button above to access training resources.
How do I print from this kiosk?
Press CTRL + P on your keyboard to open the print dialog. You can then select any printer available on this computer.
Troubleshooting
The Kiosk page isn't loading or looks frozen. What do I do?
If the page stops responding, click the End Session button in the top right and it will restart the page. If it still doesn't work, try restarting the computer.
How do I contact IT support?
Need help? Submit a request using the IT Support Form button above, or click here. For urgent issues, call (320) 762-4300.
